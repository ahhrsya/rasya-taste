# Editorial Brand

Best for portfolios, agencies, personal brands, campaign microsites, opinionated product launches, and brand pages with a strong point of view.

## Source Ideas

- an architecture monograph
- an independent magazine masthead
- a gallery exhibition catalog
- a record label release page

## Palette

Near-monochrome with one sharp accent, or a publication-like paper/ink system. Use restraint so the composition does the work.

## Type

Type hierarchy is the design. Pair a characterful display with a readable text face, or use one variable family with dramatic scale contrast.

## Signature Moves

- Masthead-like hero.
- Strong grid or deliberate broken grid.
- Pull quotes, captions, indexes, and project/product lists.
- Images treated as plates, not generic cards.
- Footer can be substantial and editorial.

## Motion

Use masked type reveals, page-load choreography, horizontal index movement, or scroll-linked captions. Keep it typographic and purposeful.

## Avoid

- decorative editorial gestures with no content
- centered portfolio card grids
- fake sophistication through tiny unreadable text
- too many font families
