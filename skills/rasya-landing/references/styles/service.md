# Service

Best for healthcare, professional services, local services, consultancies, education, clinics, and trust-heavy businesses.

## Source Ideas

- a calm clinic wayfinding system
- an architectural service brochure
- a local expert profile photographed with documentary restraint

## Palette

Light or dark is allowed. Use one industry-tied accent and restrained surfaces. Trust comes from clarity, not blandness.

## Type

Approachable sans, moderate weights, generous line height. Use mono only for stats or operational details.

## Signature Moves

- Hero explains what the service does and where/for whom.
- Contact or booking action is visible early.
- Services are grouped by decision logic, not generic cards.
- Process is concrete and reassuring.
- Testimonials or outcomes are specific, never fake.

## Motion

Keep motion calm: reveal, subtle image parallax, form feedback. Avoid cinematic scroll unless the service brand is unusually expressive.

## Avoid

- generic smiling stock photos
- fake reviews
- overdesigned UI that hides contact path
- multiple competing CTAs
