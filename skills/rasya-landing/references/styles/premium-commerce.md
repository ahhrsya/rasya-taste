# Premium Commerce

Best for DTC, fashion, beauty, home, food, wellness, jewelry, and object-led ecommerce.

## Source Ideas

- a chrome-and-smoke beauty counter
- a Japanese packaging insert with precise product diagrams
- an auction catalog for a single desirable object
- a cold luxury product shoot on black glass

## Palette

Do not default to beige luxury. Rotate into colder and sharper worlds:

- smoke, chrome, off-black, one saturated product color
- bone, forest, amber, ink
- cobalt, cream, graphite
- monochrome with one electric product accent

## Type

Use an editorial display face only when the product world supports it. Otherwise use a refined sans with strong spacing discipline.

## Signature Moves

- Product is visible in the first viewport.
- Material details get their own section.
- Variants are scannable, not buried.
- Specs use real tables or comparison rows.
- Image crops are art-directed separately for desktop and mobile.

## Motion

Use product reveal, texture shimmer, scroll-linked detail zoom, or a subtle WebM material loop. Avoid gimmicky movement around purchase CTAs.

## Avoid

- generic cream/brass/espresso palette
- stock lifestyle photos that hide the product
- huge decorative hero with no buying path
- fake scarcity
