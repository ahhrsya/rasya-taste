# Conversion

Best for ecommerce SaaS, growth tools, Shopify apps, upsell products, lead generation, and offer-driven landing pages.

## Source Ideas

- a direct-response product page redesigned with editorial restraint
- a Shopify analytics dashboard photographed as a campaign object
- a clean checkout flow with one highlighted revenue lever

## Palette

Neutral base plus one commercial accent. The accent owns CTAs, checks, active pricing, and key chart marks.

Avoid random second accents. Conversion pages need decision clarity.

## Type

Use a confident grotesque display with a quiet body face. Numbers should use tabular figures.

## Signature Moves

- Hero headline includes the concrete outcome.
- One believable UI/product mockup sits above the fold.
- Proof appears before explanation.
- Pricing or offer section has one clear recommendation.
- Case study section ties metric, context, and quote together.

## Avoid

- fake exact metrics
- three identical feature cards
- vague "all-in-one platform" copy
- CTA labels that do not name the action
