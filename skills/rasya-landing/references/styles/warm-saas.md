# Warm SaaS

Best for productivity, collaboration, consumer SaaS, growth tools, and approachable B2B.

## Source Ideas

- a colorful desk planner translated into software
- a friendly product onboarding flow
- a referral campaign board with tactile cards

## Palette

Warm gradient bloom or soft tinted base with one saturated accent. Keep the accent consistent.

## Type

Use a friendly grotesque display and a calmer body face. Keep headings confident but not corporate-heavy.

## Signature Moves

- Asymmetric hero with floating UI cards.
- One hero feature card visually differs from support cards.
- Playful markers are allowed when the brand voice supports them.
- Screenshots look like actual product states.
- Social proof feels human, not enterprise-logo-heavy.

## Motion

Use gentle card physics, task completion feedback, staggered UI entry, or small product-state transitions.

## Avoid

- childish colors for serious productivity
- centered generic SaaS hero
- equal feature card rows
- emojis unless they fit the brand
